TENET UL212-E / UL212NT-E — Protocol 17 (Modbus RTU)
Source: ul212-e-modbus-17-user-manual.pdf (ATO, “Modbus protocol 17 Testing in PC with UL212”)

Configure protocol 17 in the UL212 Fuel APP or PC client.
Serial: 9600, net address 1.

FC03 Read Holding Registers — one register per command (qty 0001).

Addr    Name           Manual command
0x0100  fuel height    01 03 01 00 00 01 85 F6
0x0102  tilt angle     01 03 01 02 00 01 24 36

Manual replies (CRC verified on the six data bytes):
  height  01 03 01 00 04 08 47 30    value 0x0408 = 1032 raw → 103.2 mm at scale 0.1
  tilt    01 03 01 02 90 06 09 F4    value 0x9006 (raw; unit not stated)

ATO labels 00 01 as “net address”; on the wire that field is Modbus quantity=1.
The documented reply echoes the register address (01 00 / 01 02) instead of a
standard FC03 byte-count (02). If a standard master NACKs, poll with a raw
serial decoder using those frames.

Do not burst-read 0x0100–0x0102 (0x0101 is not documented).
Scale 0.1 mm matches Digital Matter UL212 raw = mm × 10.
